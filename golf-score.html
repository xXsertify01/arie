Stroke Play

In stroke play, each player counts the total number of strokes taken to complete the course. The player with the lowest total score wins.

  • Record the number of strokes taken for each hole in the respective column.
  • At the end of the round, sum the scores for all holes to get the total score.
  • The player with the lowest total score at the end wins.
